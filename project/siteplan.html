The BrightSide Orbit

Website Planning Document

Project Site Plan

Name: The BrightSide Orbit

Reasoning: The name creates a feeling of being surrounded by positive energy. It implies a safe orbit where good news, hope, and constructive mindset strategies constantly rotate to support the user.

Mock Domain: brightsideorbit.org

Site Purpose

The BrightSide Orbit acts as an antidote to digital doomscrolling, providing a curated space for positive content and constructive engagement. The site aims to foster a sense of community and well-being by promoting uplifting stories, useful resources, and meaningful interactions. The target audience includes students, professionals, and anyone seeking a more positive outlook on life and a hopeful mindset.

Scenarios

  • Where can I find a quick, action-based daily exercise to help improve my mood when I feel overwhelmed?
  • How can I stay informed about positive developments in my community?

Color Schema

This planning document directly utilizes the project color palette to demonstrate the branding identity:

  • Primary Color: #6190d1 (Blue)
  • Secondary Color: #ecb74d (Gold)
  • Background Color: #cdd2d6 (Light Gray)
  • Text Color: #4c4f53 (Dark Gray)

Typography

The site will utilize the following fonts to ensure a consistent and readable experience:

  • Headings: Xanh Mono (italicized for emphasis)
  • Body Text: Open Sans (for optimal readability)

The wireframe section will provide a visual representation of the site's layout and structure, ensuring a cohesive and user-friendly design.
